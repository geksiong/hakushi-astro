Studio Atlas is a fictional identity project for a shared creative workspace. Its visual system uses a simple grid, generous spacing, and a small set of colors that can be rearranged across print and digital layouts.

## The idea

The grid gives the identity a recognizable shape without making every piece look identical. Blocks can hold a photograph, a project title, or a short message while the overall composition stays consistent.
