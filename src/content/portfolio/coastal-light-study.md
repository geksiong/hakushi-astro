Coastal Light is a fictional landscape study. Broad bands of color describe the horizon, water, and shoreline with only a few details.

## What to notice

The warm sky and cool water share the same muted tones. Small highlights along the water pull the eye toward the horizon. The image is a local asset in `public/portfolio`, so the demo works without an external image service.
