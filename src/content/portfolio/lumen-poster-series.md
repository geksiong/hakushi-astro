Lumen is a fictional poster series that pairs large geometric shapes with a restrained palette. Each composition starts with one bright form and builds depth through translucent layers.

## Process

The studies began as small sketches. The final artwork keeps the strongest shapes and leaves open space around them so the colors carry the composition.

The cover shown in the listing is one crop of the series. This page can hold more images, process notes, and credits as a real portfolio project grows.
